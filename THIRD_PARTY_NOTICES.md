This skill adapts the upstream ideas of removing staged framing, imaginary objections, repetitive rhythm, inflated claims, and chatbot residue while preserving supported meaning and author voice. It adds report-specific statistical guidance, notebook integrity checks, bilingual synthetic examples, and a contribution-focused approach to redundant defensive writing.

The upstream skill cites Wikipedia's “Signs of AI writing” as background. This repository does not reproduce that Wikipedia page; the guidance and examples here are independently written or adapted from the MIT-licensed skill. The original humanizer is not a runtime dependency.

No private conversation transcript or research artifact is distributed. All numeric examples and notebook fixtures are synthetic.
